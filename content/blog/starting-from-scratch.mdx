I created my website to show my world and keep my memories. It sounds simple when you write it down, but the road to get here was anything but.

## From not knowing, to keep going

Started from scratch, I didn't know where my feet were going to lead me. No map, no manual — just a camera, a laptop, and a lot of positive thinking. Some days the work is quite minimalist, and honestly, I'm not good at that one **yet**. But I know I will make it soon.

## Why both modeling and web design

People ask why I do two very different things. For me they're the same thing: **telling a story**. In modeling, I tell it in front of the camera. In web design, I tell it behind the screen — through layout, spacing, and the small details nobody notices until they're gone.

> Every shoot, every layout, every project is a step forward.

## What's next

This blog is where I'll keep sharing the journey — the shoots, the designs, and the memories in between. Thank you for being here at the beginning.
